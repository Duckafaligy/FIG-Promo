Good questions.

Straight answers. More in the help center.

For restaurants

When can my restaurant join?

Today: sign up on the web and set up your page. It's free. FIG plans to open to diners on January 1, 2027, in Markham first, expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills.

What does FIG cost restaurants?

Nothing. Signing up, building your page, menu and deals, publishing them, scanning and your dashboard are all free, for up to 2 locations for now, with no commission on dine-in or on the bill. FIG earns only on takeout orders diners pay for in the app: it keeps 5% of the food after any deal and before tax (plus 13% HST on that fee once FIG is registered for HST), and Stripe's card fee (2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards) comes off the same payment, paid by the restaurant. Publishing and takeout both need you to finish Stripe's identity, business and bank checks; takeout also needs your HST number, two-step sign-in and the takeout switch. Stripe pays the rest out to your bank on its usual schedule, often a few business days.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan: open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming soon). Invite your staff in Settings: they scan with their own FIG sign-in.

When can I publish my page?

Setting up is free: photos, address, hours, cuisine, your menu and your deals, all saved as drafts that diners can't see. To publish them, add at least one photo and finish Stripe's identity, business and bank checks (about 5 minutes; have your ID and a bank account ready). Diners see published pages and deals when FIG plans to open to them, on January 1, 2027. An HST number only matters for takeout paid in the app, and you can add it later.

For diners

When does FIG open for diners?

January 1, 2027, in Markham first, expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills. Restaurants are setting up first. When FIG opens, people on the list get in first, in the order they joined, before it opens to everyone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the day it opens.

Is FIG free?

Yes, completely. Deals are free to claim and use, and you never pay FIG anything.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page for as long as the deal runs. Show the QR code at the counter when you order, and staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. A code works once. After staff scan it, it's used, and if the deal can be used again your phone shows a new code, so a screenshot is just "already used".

What do I pay?

Just your bill, like normal, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app; that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners anything.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Takeout orders go through the app and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, watch it get made, and pick it up. Some restaurants also take takeout at full price: tap Order takeout on their page. FIG doesn't deliver.
