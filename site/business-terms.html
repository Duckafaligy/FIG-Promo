The short version

  • Restaurants use FIG for free: your page, deals, scans and dashboard cost nothing, with no commission on the bill (up to 2 locations for now).
  • Takeout orders paid in the app: FIG keeps 5% of the food (plus HST on that fee once FIG is registered), and Stripe’s card fee comes off the same payment, paid by the restaurant.
  • Honour your live deals exactly as written.
  • Only you and the staff you invite can verify a deal.

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to restaurants in Canada that use FIG Business. They sit alongside our Terms of service and Privacy policy. By signing up, you confirm you're 18 or older and can accept them for your business.

2. Your listing

You must run a real, licensed food business at the address you give us, and keep your details accurate. Setting up is free. Your page can be published once it has at least one photo and you have finished Stripe’s checks (see below). We can pause, unpublish or remove a listing that isn’t a real restaurant or that breaks these terms.

You can set up your page, menu and deals for free and keep them as drafts. To publish them, and to turn on takeout, you must finish Stripe’s identity, business and bank checks. Stripe collects that information; FIG does not see your ID or bank details and only keeps whether and when Stripe verified your account.

3. Your deals

You choose your deals, times and daily limits. While a deal is live, you must honour it exactly as listed. Don't raise prices to cancel out a discount. If you pause a deal, keep honouring claims made before that. Ending a deal is what stops its claims.

You can let a diner use a deal more than once (up to 20 times) and set a wait between uses. Each claim keeps the rules it was made with.

4. What FIG costs

FIG is free for restaurants. Signing up, setting up your listing for all of your locations (up to 2 for now), going live, posting deals, verified scans and your dashboard cost nothing. There's no upfront payment, no monthly or yearly fee, no commission on the bill, and no per-scan or per-listing fees.

FIG earns only on takeout orders that diners pay for in the app, as explained below. Nothing is taken on dine-in or on a bill paid at your counter. FIG plans to open to diners on January 1, 2027, and can't promise how many diners you'll get.

Publishing needs only Stripe’s checks. Takeout also needs your HST number, a recent two-step sign-in code for money steps and the takeout switch. Everything else is available without them.

Stripe processes takeout payments. FIG never sees a card number.

Everything FIG offers restaurants today is included. If we ever offer a paid extra, we'll show the price and what's included before you buy anything.

Menu reading from a photo is free for every restaurant. It is limited to a few reads a day for each owner and needs a confirmed email. It can make mistakes, so you must check every line it reads before you publish, and your menu and prices are your responsibility.

Your takeout orders are paid out to you through Stripe. Setting up payouts happens inside FIG, and accepting Stripe’s terms (the Stripe Connected Account Agreement) is part of it. Your ID and bank details go straight to Stripe, never to FIG.

When a diner pays for a takeout order in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food after the deal’s discount, before tax, and Stripe’s card fee (2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards; cards from outside Canada cost more) comes off the same payment. The rest, tax included, is paid out to you, and the tax is yours to remit.

If you refund an order, in full or in part, FIG gives back its fee in proportion. Stripe keeps its card fee.

Diners choose their pickup time. If a diner picks a time you can make, accepting the order keeps that time. Accepting never moves a time the diner picked earlier.

A diner can cancel an accepted takeout order for a full refund until 20 minutes before its pickup time. You are told right away. The refund includes FIG’s fee on that order, and Stripe’s own fee is not returned. From 20 minutes before pickup the order is locked in; only you can still refund it by hand.

FIG isn’t registered for HST yet. Once it is, it will add 13% HST to its 5% fee, and we’ll tell you at least 30 days before that starts.

Your payments: every takeout payment, refund and fee is listed under Payments, where you can download them as a CSV file. It is a permanent record, kept for 7 years.

Each month you get a fee statement: a summary of FIG’s fee on your takeout orders. It is not a tax invoice until FIG is registered for HST.

5. Wrong scans and disputes

If you think a scan is wrong, message us within 14 days. We review it, and if it was a mistake we remove it.

6. Reviews

Paying FIG never changes, hides or removes reviews. You can reply to reviews in public. Don't offer anything in exchange for a review.

7. Staff

You're responsible for scans made by your team. Only owners and managers can refund takeout orders by hand. Remove access when someone leaves. People join your team only by accepting an invite you send, to one email address or by a link that expires and that you can revoke. Owners can invite managers and staff; managers can invite staff. Owners and managers need two-step sign-in turned on, and a fresh code, for money steps like payouts, refunds, handing a restaurant over and closing it.

8. Diner information

You'll see a diner's first name, last initial and the deal they used, never their mobile number or email. Reach a diner only through FIG (for example "We're waiting" on a takeout order), and only to serve that deal or order. Don't contact diners outside FIG, and handle their information as Canadian privacy law requires.

If a diner deletes their account, you keep your records of their orders and visits, shown as "Former diner".

9. Ending the agreement

You can close your business account anytime. Takeout orders already paid are paid out as usual. We can suspend or close your account if you break these terms, for example by not honouring deals or faking scans. If we close it for any other reason, nothing more is charged.

Closing a location, or your business account, deletes that location's orders and visit records from FIG. Save your takeout statements first: you need them for your own tax records.

10. Responsibility

You're responsible for your food, prices, service and safety, and for any claims about them. FIG is provided "as is". As far as the law allows, our total liability is limited to the greater of the fees FIG kept from your takeout orders in the last 12 months or $100 CAD.

11. Changes and law

If we ever charge for something, we'll give you at least 30 days' notice first. These terms follow Ontario law, and disputes go to the courts of Ontario.
