Ordering takeout on FIG, step by step: what happens to your card

You pay in the app, the restaurant accepts, and you pick up. What happens to your card at each step, when the kitchen starts, what the pickup code is for, and the total on a worked example.

Key takeaways

  • You pay online in the app at checkout. There is no paying at the counter.
  • Your card is held and charged when the restaurant accepts. If the restaurant declines, nothing is charged.
  • The kitchen starts when the restaurant accepts, so the clock for your pickup starts then, not at checkout.
  • The pickup code only confirms collection. It is not how you pay.
  • Takeout comes with a deal, unless the restaurant sets 0 deals per order, and then takeout is at full price.

The short answer

You choose a deal and the dishes, pay by card in the app at checkout, and wait for the restaurant to accept. Your card is held and charged when the restaurant accepts, and nothing is charged if it declines. The kitchen starts once it accepts, and you pick the order up with a pickup code that only confirms you have collected it. FIG is free for diners, and it is open in Markham now at www.figpromo.ca/app.

Before you order

You can browse places, menus and live deals as a guest, with no account. To order, you need an account. Diners must be 14 or older. Location is optional, and it never limits a deal, so you can look at a restaurant across town just as easily as one nearby. Browsing as a guest covers what you can do before you sign up.

Not every restaurant offers takeout. A restaurant can turn takeout on only after it has added its HST number and finished payouts, so the option appears on the places that are set up for it.

The steps, in order

StepWhat you doWhat happens to your card
1. ChoosePick a place, a deal and your dishes from the menuNothing yet
2. PayCheck out in the appYour card is held
3. WaitThe restaurant looks at the orderStill held
4a. AcceptedThe kitchen startsYour card is charged
4b. DeclinedOrder does not go aheadNothing is charged
5. Pick upGo to the restaurant and show your pickup codeAlready paid

There is no paying at the counter. The pickup code confirms that you collected the order. It is not a way to pay, and it is not the code you show for a dine-in deal.

Why your card is held first

The restaurant decides whether it can make your order. On a busy night it may be short of an ingredient, or it may need more time. Holding the card at checkout and charging only on acceptance means you are not paid up for food nobody has started. If the restaurant declines, nothing is charged. A declined or refunded order, from the restaurant's side shows the same steps from its point of view.

Because the kitchen starts only once the restaurant accepts, you should leave for pickup after you see that it has, not before. An order made when you are already at the door is the safest way to get hot food.

What the total looks like

Here is an example with round numbers. Ontario HST is 13%, worked out on the price after the restaurant's own discount, the same way a store coupon lowers the price before tax. This is general information, not tax advice.

Two $15 mains with a $5-off deal (example)
  • Two mains: 2 × $15 = $30.00.
  • Deal: $30.00 − $5.00 = $25.00.
  • HST at 13%: 0.13 × $25.00 = $3.25.
  • You pay $25.00 + $3.25 = $28.25.

These are example prices. The real total on your screen will depend on the restaurant's menu, its deal and its rules, so read it before you pay.

Compare that with the same order at full price: $30.00 plus $3.90 of HST is $33.90. In this example the deal saves $5.65 once tax is counted. Takeout or dine-in with a deal compares both ways to eat.

Deals and takeout

Takeout comes with a deal, unless the restaurant sets 0 deals per order. In that case takeout is at full price. A restaurant can also limit how many deals one order can use, so a large order may not stack discounts. A diner can claim a deal once a day, so plan which meal gets the deal. How to compare deals helps with the choice.

Pickup

Go to the restaurant when it has accepted and the food should be ready. If the restaurant added prep time, allow for it. Show your pickup code to the staff. It only confirms collection, so it does not matter that the payment was made earlier.

  • Check the bag against your order before you leave.
  • If you have an allergy, call the restaurant after you order, since you cannot ask a server at a table. What to ask about diets and allergies has a short list. This is general information, not medical advice.
  • If something is wrong, speak to the restaurant straight away while the order is in front of you.

If you change your mind

Because the card is held until the restaurant accepts, the moment to change your mind is early. If you have just paid and realise you picked the wrong dish, contact the restaurant straight away, before it accepts, while it can still say no. After the kitchen has started, the food is being made for you, so the restaurant has to decide what it can do. The contact page at www.figpromo.ca/contact is there for problems with the app itself, but the restaurant is the one holding your food.

The simple rule is to read the cart before you pay. Check the dishes, the number of each, the deal and the total. It takes ten seconds, and it is the easiest way to avoid a mix-up.

Takeout for a group or a family

A single pickup for several people is one trip, one payment and one person carrying the bags. It works best when everyone agrees on dishes before checkout, since the order is paid in one go.

Splitting a pickup four ways (example)
  • Four mains at $15 each: 4 × $15 = $60.00.
  • A $5-off deal: $60.00 − $5.00 = $55.00.
  • HST at 13%: 0.13 × $55.00 = $7.15.
  • Total $62.15. Split four ways, that is $15.5375, so about $15.54 each.

These are example prices. Each person can send you their share by whatever method you already use with friends. FIG does not split the bill for you.

A short checklist

  1. Browse as a guest, then make an account when you want to order.
  2. Choose a deal and your dishes. Read the total.
  3. Pay in the app. Your card is held.
  4. Wait for the restaurant to accept. That is when your card is charged and the kitchen starts.
  5. Pick up, show the pickup code and check the bag.

Questions

How do I pay for takeout on FIG?

Online, in the app, at checkout. There is no paying at the counter.

When is my card charged?

Your card is held at checkout and charged when the restaurant accepts the order.

What happens if the restaurant declines my order?

Nothing is charged.

When does the kitchen start?

When the restaurant accepts the order.

What is the pickup code for?

It only confirms that you collected the order. It is not how you pay.

Can I order takeout without a deal?

Only if the restaurant sets 0 deals per order. Then takeout is at full price. Otherwise takeout comes with a deal.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Guests can look around, but you need an account to claim, order, save or review. FIG is free for diners.
