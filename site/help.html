Eating out with FIG

Claiming, codes, paying and takeout.

Is FIG free?

Yes, completely. Deals are free to claim and use.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page for as long as the deal runs. Show the QR code at the counter when you order, and staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. A code works once. After staff scan it, it's used, and if the deal can be used again your phone shows a new code, so a screenshot is just "already used".

What do I pay?

Just your bill, like normal, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app; that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners anything.

How do reviews work?

Only diners whose visit was scanned can review, so every review comes from a real visit. Rate a visit right after it, or later from Past visits on your Deals page.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Takeout orders go through the app and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, watch it get made, and pick it up. Some restaurants also take takeout at full price: tap Order takeout on their page. FIG doesn't deliver.

When can I pick up my order?

You choose the time, from the earliest time the restaurant can make it up to 24 hours ahead, while the restaurant is open.

Can I cancel a takeout order?

Yes, for a full refund at any time before the restaurant accepts it, and after that until 20 minutes before your pickup time. From 20 minutes before pickup the restaurant may already be making your food, so you can no longer cancel through FIG. A cancelled order is refunded in full to the card you paid with; your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What if a restaurant won't honour a deal?

Report the visit in the app. A real person looks at every report and replies within 24 hours.

Running deals on FIG

Cost, scanning, deals and takeout.

What does FIG cost?

Nothing. Signing up, setting up your page, going live, posting deals, scanning and your dashboard are all free, for up to 2 locations for now, with no commission on dine-in or on the bill. FIG earns only on takeout orders diners pay for in the app: it keeps 5% of the food after any deal and before tax (plus 13% HST on that fee once FIG is registered for HST), and Stripe's card fee (2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards) comes off the same payment, paid by the restaurant. Takeout needs your HST number and finished Stripe payouts. Stripe pays the rest out to your bank on its usual schedule, often a few business days.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan: open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming soon). Invite your staff in Settings: they scan with their own FIG sign-in.

What if a code won't scan?

Tap Enter code and type the 8 characters under the diner's QR. It runs the same check as a scan.

How do I post a deal?

In the app, open Deals and tap New deal. Pick the dishes (or the whole order, for dine-in), the offer, the days and hours, and check it the way diners will see it. You can pause it anytime, or post an ended deal again in one tap.

When does my page go live?

Setting up is free: photos, address, hours and cuisine. Add at least one photo and publish. Diners see your page and deals when FIG plans to open to them, on January 1, 2027. An HST number and bank details only matter for takeout paid in the app, and you can add them later.

Can diners order takeout?

Yes. Add your menu: first the sections, then the dishes in each. Turn on takeout and pick how many deals one order can use: 1, 2, 3, more or no limit, or 0 to take orders at full price. You scan their pickup code when they collect. The diner's card is charged when you accept the order; decline it and nothing is charged.

Can I refund an order?

Yes, owners and managers can: open the order and tap Refund, for all of it or part. FIG gives back its fee in proportion; Stripe keeps its card fee.

Can a diner cancel a takeout order?

Yes. A diner can cancel an accepted order for a full refund until 20 minutes before its pickup time, and you are told right away. The refund includes FIG's fee on that order, and Stripe's own fee is not returned. From 20 minutes before pickup the order is locked in; only you can still refund it by hand. Diners choose their pickup time, and accepting an order never moves a time they picked.

What if a scan looks wrong?

Message us from Help within 14 days. We review it, and if it was a mistake we remove it.

Can I pause takeout in a rush?

Yes. Tap Takeout: On on your dashboard or Orders to pause new orders for 20 or 40 minutes or until tomorrow's opening, or to add prep time. Orders already in aren't touched, and it comes back on by itself.

How do I stop?

There's nothing to cancel, because FIG is free for restaurants. You can pause your deals anytime, turn takeout off, or close your account in Settings.
