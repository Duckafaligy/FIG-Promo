Do you need Stripe to go live on FIG? What works without it

A restaurant can go live on FIG with its page, menu and dine-in deals without Stripe. Stripe's checks are needed only for takeout paid in the app. Here is what that means, with the order math.

Key takeaways

  • No Stripe is needed to go live. Your page, menu and dine-in deals can be live as soon as they are ready, with at least one photo.
  • Stripe's identity, business and bank checks are needed only to turn on takeout paid in the app.
  • Stripe, not FIG, checks ID and holds ID and bank details. FIG never sees them.
  • Takeout also needs your HST number, and owners and managers need sign-in security for money steps.
  • On a $25 takeout order, example fees leave about $22.63 of the food. Dine-in deals carry no fee to FIG.

The short answer

No. You do not need Stripe to go live on FIG. A restaurant publishes its page, menu and dine-in deals as soon as they are ready, with at least one photo, and there is no Stripe, no ID check and no payment setup in the way. Stripe comes in only if you want to turn on takeout paid in the app.

This post separates the two jobs, so you can start with the quick one today and decide on takeout when you are ready.

What you can do without Stripe

Everything below works without any payment setup. Restaurants are completely free on FIG: signing up, setting up the page, going live, posting deals, scanning and the dashboard cost nothing.

  • Your page. Name, photos, hours and a menu with photos and sections.
  • Dine-in deals. 2 for 1, percent off, dollar off, or free with a dish, live now or scheduled, with uses per diner and an optional wait between uses.
  • Scanning. Staff scan the diner's code at the restaurant. The diner pays you at the table or counter as usual.
  • The dashboard. Results deal by deal, plus reviews and your replies.
  • Your team. Staff join by accepting an invite from the owner, as Owner, Manager or Staff.

If you are starting from nothing, keeping your page current covers hours and menu, and your first week live covers what to do once it is up.

What Stripe is for

Stripe is the payment company behind takeout. When a diner pays online in the app, Stripe handles the card. Because money moves, Stripe asks for identity, business and bank details before payouts can start. Stripe, not FIG, checks ID and holds ID and bank details. FIG never sees them.

That is the only reason Stripe is in the picture. A dine-in deal moves no money through FIG: the diner shows a code, staff scan it, and the bill is settled with you directly.

What takeout needs

You can turn takeout on only after it has finished Stripe's checks and payouts and added its HST number. Three things to have ready:

FIG sends no texts, so sign-in security uses an authenticator app or a code by email. Not sure whether you must register for HST? HST registration for small restaurants is general information, not tax advice.

What an order leaves you, with the math

When takeout is on, diners pay in the app at checkout. The card is held and charged when the restaurant accepts, and nothing is charged if the restaurant declines. Two fees come off a takeout order: Stripe's card fee of 2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards (paid by the restaurant), and FIG's 5% of the food after any deal and before tax. FIG takes nothing on dine-in.

Check the $25 row: Stripe takes 2.9% of $28.25, which is $0.82, plus $0.30, so $1.12. FIG takes 5% of $25, which is $1.25. $25.00 − $1.12 − $1.25 = $22.63. The HST is paid out with the rest and is yours to remit. Fees are rounded to the cent, and FIG's fee has HST added once FIG is registered for HST.

A sensible order of work

  1. Go live first. Add at least one photo, your hours, a menu and one dine-in deal for a quiet hour.
  2. Watch the results for a week or two. Reading your deal results shows what to look at.
  3. Then decide on takeout. If you have a few dishes that travel well, start Stripe's checks.
  4. Set the takeout rules. Takeout comes with a deal unless you set 0 deals per order, which makes takeout full price. You can pause takeout and add prep time. Takeout during a rush covers those.

Nothing forces the order, but starting with dine-in means you are earning from deals while the takeout paperwork is still in progress.

What diners see when takeout is on

For a diner, takeout is a different path from claiming a dine-in deal. They pay online in the app at checkout. The card is held and charged when the restaurant accepts, and nothing is charged if the restaurant declines. The kitchen starts when the restaurant accepts. There is no paying at the counter, and the pickup code only confirms collection. Ordering takeout on FIG shows the diner's side, which is useful when you train staff to hand over an order.

Where to find your money later

Owners and managers see a Payouts page. It shows what the restaurant kept from takeout so far and this month, then lists every takeout payment, refund and fee by month, and it downloads as a CSV file. With no takeout it shows $0.00. Bank payout dates are in the restaurant's own Stripe account. Reading your FIG Payments page goes through it for bookkeeping.

Questions to ask before you turn takeout on

Takeout is a separate business decision from going live, so it is worth a few minutes of thought. Ask yourself:

  • Which dishes travel well? Food that holds its heat and shape for ten minutes in a bag is a good start. Leave the rest off.
  • Who will run it during service? Takeout starts in the kitchen when you accept the order, so someone needs to watch for new orders. You can pause takeout and add prep time when it gets busy.
  • Do you want full-price takeout? Set 0 deals per order and takeout is at full price. Otherwise, takeout comes with a deal.
  • Is your HST number ready? It is needed before takeout can go on, along with Stripe's checks and payouts.
  • Who holds the sign-in security? Owners and managers need it for payouts, taking takeout live and refunds, so set it up on a phone you will have to hand.

If most answers are "not yet", that is fine. Your dine-in deals keep working while you decide.

Common worries

  • "I do not want to hand over my bank details to a new app." You do not have to to go live. If you turn on takeout later, those details go to Stripe, not FIG.
  • "I only do dine-in." Then you may never need Stripe. Dine-in deals are complete without it.
  • "Will diners be confused if takeout is off?" Diners see what each restaurant offers. A restaurant without takeout simply has dine-in deals.

Questions about your own setup can go to FIG on the contact page.

Questions

Do I need Stripe to put my restaurant on FIG?

No. You can go live with your page, menu and dine-in deals as soon as they are ready, with at least one photo, with no Stripe, no ID check and no payment setup.

When do I need Stripe?

Only to turn on takeout paid in the app. Stripe's identity, business and bank checks and payouts, plus your HST number, are needed first.

Does FIG see my ID or bank details?

No. Stripe, not FIG, checks ID and holds ID and bank details.

What does FIG cost a restaurant?

Signing up, going live, posting deals, scanning and the dashboard cost nothing. FIG earns only on takeout orders paid in the app, keeping 5% of the food after any deal and before tax. It takes nothing from a dine-in bill.

Who pays Stripe's fee?

The restaurant. Stripe's card fee of 2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards comes off the takeout payment.

Who needs sign-in security?

Owners and managers need it for money steps: payouts, taking takeout live and refunds. It uses an authenticator app or a code by email.

Can I start with dine-in and add takeout later?

Yes. Go live with dine-in deals first, and turn takeout on once Stripe's checks, payouts and your HST number are done.
