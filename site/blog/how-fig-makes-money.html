FIG is free for restaurants. Here's how we make money

Listing, deals, scans and chats are free on FIG, with no commission on dine-in. FIG is paid by the optional FIG Premium ($50 a year) and 5% of the food on takeout orders diners pay for in the app.

By the FIG team · Markham, Ontario · 2 min read

Key points

  • FIG is free for restaurants: listing, unlimited deals, every scan and chats. No commission on dine-in.
  • FIG makes money two ways: the optional FIG Premium ($50 a year), and 5% of the food on takeout orders diners pay for in the app.
  • FIG Premium adds unlimited locations, a deal announcement every 2 hours to diners who crave your food, area insights, your menu typed up from a photo and a badge.
  • Insights are free: your views, claims and the diners who came in, day by day.

What's free

Everything you need to bring diners in: your page, unlimited everyday and flash deals, scanning diners at the counter on any phone, tablet or computer, takeout on your deals, chats with diners and a dashboard of what's working. No commission on dine-in, no fee per diner, no monthly fee.

A diner who uses your deal pays you, at your counter, the way they always do.

The one fee: when a diner orders takeout and pays in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food (before tax), and Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment. The rest, tax included, is paid out to you.

How FIG makes money

Two ways, and both are up to you. FIG Premium is $50 a year for your whole account. And when a diner orders takeout and pays in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food. A diner who walks in with your deal costs you nothing.

FIG Premium

For $50 a year, one plan covers every location on your account: unlimited locations (free accounts have up to 2), a deal announcement every 2 hours to nearby diners whose cravings match your food and to everyone who saved you, area insights, your menu typed up from a photo, and a FIG Premium badge on your page. The badge never moves you up in search or on the map.

You see what you get

Insights are free for every restaurant: views of your page, claims, the diners who came in and who came back, day by day, and how each deal is doing. With Premium, area insights add when diners near you claim deals, what they crave and what's trending nearby.

What paying FIG never does

  • Change your deal or its price
  • Move you up in search or on the map
  • Hide, remove or reorder reviews
  • Share any diner's details with you

Questions

How much does FIG cost a restaurant?

Nothing for dine-in deals. FIG Premium is optional, $50 a year. On takeout orders diners pay for in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food (before tax).

Is there a contract?

No. Premium renews yearly; cancel anytime and it stays on until the end of the year you paid for.
