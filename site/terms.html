Terms of service

The short version

  • FIG is free for you, and for restaurants. Restaurants can pay to sponsor a spot, always labelled Sponsored.
  • A deal counts once staff scan your QR at the restaurant.
  • One account per person. No sharing codes or faking visits.

1. Who we are

FIG is a local deals app run by FIG Technologies Inc. in Ontario, Canada ("FIG", "we", "us"). These terms cover the FIG app and website. By creating an account, you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Your account

You need an account to use FIG, and you must be 14 or older. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian that it's okay. Use your real name, an email and a mobile number, each confirmed with a code. One account per person and per mobile number. Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

3. How deals work

Deals are offered by restaurants, not by FIG. Each deal shows its own rules (times, limits, dine-in only, and so on) before you claim it.

• Claiming holds a deal for you for 24 hours.

• At the restaurant, open your QR code. It refreshes every 5 minutes.

• The deal is used once staff scan it. That scan is a "redemption".

Restaurants can change or end a deal at any time. A deal isn't guaranteed until staff scan it. Takeout orders are paid to the restaurant when you pick up. Deals that allow takeout are applied to your order; dine-in only deals are not. If you don't collect an order, the restaurant can report a no-show. You can contest it. Two no-shows within 2 months pause takeout: for 2 weeks the first time, then 2 months, 6 months and 1 year if it happens again before your record clears, and after that takeout is off for good. A no-show counts for 2 months and stays on your record, which restaurants can see when you order.

4. Play fair

Restaurants honour every deal in good faith, so we protect them from abuse. Don't:

• make more than one account

• share, screenshot or sell QR codes

• fake visits or redeem without buying

• use bots, scripts or location spoofing

• harass restaurant staff or other diners

If we see this, we may cancel claims or suspend your account.

5. Sponsored spots

FIG is free for diners and restaurants. Restaurants can pay FIG to advertise a deal or their restaurant. Paid spots are always labelled Sponsored, and tapping the label says why you see it. Paying never changes a deal, its price or its reviews, and restaurants never get your details from it.

6. Restaurants and your food

FIG doesn't run any restaurant. Food, prices, service, safety and allergens are the restaurant's responsibility. Dietary filters are a guide only. Always tell staff about allergies before you order.

7. What you send us

If you send us photos, reports or feedback, you still own them. You let us use them to run and improve FIG. Don't send anything that isn't yours or breaks the law. Reviews must be about your own scanned visit and honest. They're public under your first name and last initial. We can remove reviews that are abusive, fake or off-topic.

8. Leaving FIG

You can delete your account anytime in Profile → Account & password. We can suspend or close accounts that break these terms. If we do, we'll tell you why unless the law or safety stops us.

9. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep FIG accurate and running, but it's provided "as is". As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to the greater of what you paid us in the last 12 months or $50 CAD. Nothing here removes your rights under Ontario consumer protection law.

10. Changes to these terms

If we make an important change, we'll tell you in the app at least 14 days before it starts. If you keep using FIG after that, the new terms apply.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there. Disputes go to the courts of Ontario.
