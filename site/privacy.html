The short version

  • We collect what we need to show you deals nearby and check that redemptions are real.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Restaurants only see what they need to verify your visit.
  • Location is used while you use FIG, and for alerts only if you turn them on.
  • You can download or delete your data anytime.
  • If you delete your account, restaurants keep their order and visit records without your name.

1. Who's responsible

FIG, based in Markham, Ontario, Canada, is responsible for your personal information. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA). Our Privacy Officer, Brendan Lau, answers questions sent with Message us in the app, or from the contact page at www.figpromo.ca/contact.

2. What we collect

Account: name, username, email (confirmed with a code), optional mobile number (a contact number only: never verified, never used to sign in or send codes, never used to market to you), photo, and your password (stored scrambled, never readable).

Taste: cravings, budget, dietary needs and home area from Profile setup.

Location: your precise location while you use FIG, to show nearby deals. It's optional: you can find, claim and use every deal with it off. If you tap I'm here on a takeout order, we keep how far you were from the restaurant with that order. If you turn on arrival reminders, also when you arrive at a restaurant where you have a claimed deal.

Activity: claims, verified visits, searches, takeout orders, and the reviews and photos you post.

Device: phone model, app version, notification token and crash reports.

Terms: which version of our terms you accepted, and when.

Two-step sign-in: if you turn it on, the key that links FIG to your authenticator app (the codes it makes stay on your phone), or the email address we send codes to. When we send you a code we keep only a scrambled copy of it for 10 minutes. Backup codes are kept scrambled too.

Support: messages and reports you send us.

Emails: when you join the waitlist, your email, that you agreed to emails, when you confirmed your address and whether you have unsubscribed. A do-not-email list (the address, why it is on the list and the date), so an unsubscribe, a bounced email or a spam complaint is always honoured. A log of the emails we send, which holds a scrambled version of your address instead of the address, the kind of email, when, and whether it went through.

Payments (restaurants): a permanent record of each payment made through FIG: amounts, HST, FIG's fee, Stripe's fee, order numbers, dates and Stripe reference numbers. It has no card numbers, names or emails.

Stripe verification status: when Stripe verified your account and whether payments were later paused. Stripe collects and keeps the identity and bank details you give it, under Stripe’s own privacy policy.

Team invitations: the email address a restaurant invites, the role, and when the invite ends.

Ownership requests: if you tell FIG that a restaurant already on FIG is yours, we keep your name, role, email and note. Only you and FIG can read it. It is kept after an account is closed, with the link to the account removed.

Receipts: for takeout, your confirmed email is sent to Stripe so it can email your receipt.

How you found FIG: the tag on the link you came from (a creator’s link or a flyer’s QR code, for example), noted once when you join the waitlist, sign up or add a restaurant, and your answer to “How did you hear about FIG?” if you give one (you can skip it). We use them only to see which outreach works. Restaurants never see them.

In your browser: your sign-in and the app's saved state (so you stay signed in), which link brought you to FIG (until you close the tab), whether FIG is open to diners, and an offline copy of the website's pages. No advertising cookies. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies for up to 2 years; if you decline, it sets none. Vercel Web Analytics sets no cookies.

3. How we use it

Show deals and places near you

Verify redemptions and stop fraud

Keep a record of your visits, so you can rate them and find places again

Personalise Home (you can turn this off)

Send the alerts and emails you chose

Emails you need because of something you did: sign-in and two-step codes, security notices (a changed password or email), order updates and receipts, team invites, and replies to your messages. These are not marketing, so unsubscribing does not stop them

Marketing emails (for example, the message that FIG is open) only if you said yes. When you join the waitlist we email you once to confirm your address, and we send marketing only to addresses that are confirmed. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, says who we are and shows our mailing address. You can also turn them off anytime in Account & password

Answer support requests and improve FIG

FIG doesn't show ads for now. We don't sell your information or show you third-party ads based on it.

4. Dietary needs

Dietary needs can say something about your health or beliefs, so we treat them as sensitive. They're only used to filter places and deals for you, and they're never shared, with restaurants or anyone else.

5. What restaurants see

When staff scan your QR, the restaurant sees your first name and last initial, the deal, and the time. Their dashboard shows totals, like "42 redemptions this week", not who you are. Restaurants never see your email or location. When you order takeout, they see how many of your takeout orders in the last 12 months you picked up or missed, nothing else. They never see your mobile number, not even for a takeout order: they reach you only through FIG, for example "We're waiting" on a takeout order. Reviews you post are public and show your first name and last initial.

6. Companies that help us

These companies process data for us, only to run FIG, under contracts that protect it:

Supabase: database and sign-in

Vercel: hosting, and anonymous visit counts for the website (Vercel Web Analytics: no cookies, no personal profile)

Google Analytics: website visit statistics (pages viewed, approximate location, device type), using cookies only if you accept them; see the Cookies page

Resend: sends FIG's emails from FIG's own address, so it gets your email address and what the email says, and tells us whether the email was delivered

Stripe: card payments for takeout orders, and checking and paying restaurants. To set up a restaurant’s account, FIG shares its business name, address, phone and email with Stripe. ID and bank details go straight to Stripe and never pass through FIG; Stripe keeps them under its own privacy policy. FIG keeps only whether and when Stripe verified the account.

Komoot (Photon): the address search when you or a restaurant look up an address. What you type, and your internet address, go to its servers in Germany

OpenFreeMap (map tiles), jsDelivr (code the pages load) and Unsplash (some pictures on Home): they see your internet address, as any website does, so they can send it

Apple and Google: sign-in, maps, notifications

7. Where your data lives

Some of these companies store data outside Canada, mostly in the United States. There, it's protected by our contracts but also covered by local law. Address searches go to Komoot in Germany, where the European Union's privacy law applies.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we erase your personal information within 30 days, and it's gone from our backup copies within 60 days.

Restaurants keep the records of your takeout orders and visits for their tax and accounting records, for as long as they're on FIG. Payments are also recorded by Stripe, our payment processor. When you delete your account, your name on those records is replaced with "Former diner", and your notes on dishes and your distance from the restaurant are removed.

If you tick "Keep a way to bring back my past visits and orders" when you delete, we also keep a one-way code made from your email (not the email itself) for 12 months. If you sign up again with the same confirmed email in that time, you can choose to bring back those orders and visits, or start fresh, which deletes the saved link. Your saved places, claims and reviews can't come back. If you don't tick it, the records can't be linked to you again.

Payment records of takeout payments made through FIG are kept for 7 years, for tax and accounting, and stay after an account is closed. They hold no name, email or card number. An email address that a restaurant invited is deleted within 60 days after the invite ends.

The do-not-email list keeps only the address, the reason and the date, for as long as it is needed to honour your choice. That includes after you delete your account, because deleting the address would let the emails start again. The log of emails we send is deleted after 12 months.

9. Your choices

See or fix your info in Profile

Download it in Privacy & terms

Turn off location, alerts or personalised picks

Delete your account at any time

No account (on the waitlist, or you wrote to us)? Ask for a copy of your information, a correction or its deletion through the contact page (choose Privacy or my data). We answer within 30 days

Not happy with our answer? You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. Keeping it safe

We encrypt data in transit, scramble passwords and limit who on our team can access data. You can turn on two-step sign-in, so signing in also needs a code from an authenticator app or an email. FIG does not send text messages. No system is perfect. If a breach could put you at risk, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.

11. Age

FIG is for people 14 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 14 has an account, we delete it.

12. Changes

If we change how we use your information in an important way, we'll tell you in the app first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

The waitlist

While FIG opens to restaurants first, diners can join a waitlist, here on the website or in the FIG app. We keep your email, your school if you add one (in the app: picked from a list of local high schools, colleges and universities built into the app, or typed in; it only helps us see which schools to reach first), that you agreed to “I agree to get emails from FIG and can unsubscribe at any time.” and when (joining needs it), when you joined, and which link brought you, if any. We email you once to confirm your address (you tap the link in that email), and then to say FIG is open in Markham. We send no marketing to an address until it is confirmed. If you unsubscribe, the emails stop and you keep your place. Every marketing email comes from FIG, Markham, Ontario, shows our mailing address and has an unsubscribe link that works in one click: you can withdraw your consent anytime, through that link or by messaging us. We keep the address on a do-not-email list after you unsubscribe, so it stays honoured. To stop people flooding the list, we briefly count tries from your internet address. If you make a FIG account instead, your account is your place on the list. We never sell the list or share it with restaurants or advertisers.
