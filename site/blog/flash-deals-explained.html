What are flash deals, and how do they work on FIG?

Flash deals are short, real-time restaurant deals posted during quiet hours. Here's how to catch them in Markham.

By the FIG team · Markham, Ontario · 2 min read

Key points

  • A flash deal is a short deal a restaurant posts right now, for a quiet hour.
  • The timer is set by the restaurant and it's real: when it ends, the deal is gone.
  • Live ones show at the top of Search and on the map, with the time left.

What a flash deal is

Everyday deals run on a schedule, like every weekday afternoon. A flash deal is different: a restaurant posts it on the spot, because it has empty tables right now. It usually lasts an hour or two.

How to catch one

Open Search: flash deals that are live near you sit at the top under Live now near you, with the time left. They also stand out on the map.

Claim it, then go

Claim the flash deal in one tap and head over. Show your code at the counter. Like every FIG code, it refreshes every 5 minutes so it can't be screenshotted and reused.

For restaurant owners

Posting a flash deal takes a few seconds: pick the deal, the length and an optional cap. It's free, like every scan. How FIG works for restaurants.

Questions

How long do flash deals last?

The restaurant sets the length, usually an hour or two. The timer you see in the app is the real one.

Will FIG message me about flash deals?

Not on its own. Restaurants with FIG Premium can announce a deal to diners in their area, at most every 2 hours, and you can turn those off in Deal alerts.
