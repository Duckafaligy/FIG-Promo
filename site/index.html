Good questions.

Straight answers. More in the help center.

For restaurants

When can my restaurant join?

Today: sign up on the web and set up your page. Setting up is free. Your page and deals go live when your plan does (a founding deposit before FIG opens to diners). FIG plans to open to diners on January 1, 2027, in Markham first, expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills.

What does FIG cost restaurants?

One plan for all your restaurants: $29.99 a month or $299.99 a year from the day FIG opens to diners, before tax, for your pages, deals, scans and insights, with no commission on dine-in. Before launch, founding restaurants lock in less: $9.99 a month for the first 12 months, or $59.99 for the first year, paid now as a deposit (refundable within 14 days, or in full if FIG hasn't opened to diners by March 1, 2027 and your plan hasn't started). On takeout orders diners pay for in the app, FIG also keeps 5% of the food (before tax), and Stripe's card fee (2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards) comes off the same payment. Stripe pays the rest out to your bank on its usual schedule, often a few business days.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan: open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming soon). Invite your staff in Settings: they scan with their own FIG sign-in.

When does my page go live?

Setting up is free: photos, address, hours and cuisine. Your page and deals go live when your plan does (a founding deposit before FIG opens to diners). FIG plans to open to diners on January 1, 2027. An HST number and bank details only matter for takeout paid in the app, and you can add them later.

For diners

When does FIG open for diners?

January 1, 2027, in Markham first, expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills. Restaurants are setting up first. When FIG opens, people on the list get in first, in the order they joined, before it opens to everyone. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the day it opens.

Is FIG free?

Yes, completely. Deals are free to claim and use, and you never pay FIG anything.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page for as long as the deal runs. Show the QR code at the counter when you order, and staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. Your code changes every 5 minutes, so only the live code on your phone works.

What do I pay?

Just your bill, like normal, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app; that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners anything.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Takeout orders go through the app and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, watch it get made, and pick it up. Some restaurants also take takeout at full price: tap Order takeout on their page. FIG doesn't deliver.
