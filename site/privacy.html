Waitlist privacy

This covers the waitlist on this website. The FIG app has its own Privacy Policy inside the app.

Who we are

FIG is run by FIG Technologies Inc., Markham, Ontario, Canada. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and anti-spam law (CASL).

What we collect

  • Your email address or mobile number, whichever you give us.
  • Whether you're a diner or a restaurant, your restaurant's name if you add it, and the area you picked.
  • When you joined and which link brought you here (for example, Instagram).

Why

To tell you when FIG launches near you and to send you FIG news and deals. If you own a restaurant, to contact you about setting it up. We only message you because you ticked the consent box.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't share them with restaurants or advertisers.

Unsubscribe or delete

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text. To see or delete what we hold about you, email privacy@fig.app.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until the waitlist closes after launch.
