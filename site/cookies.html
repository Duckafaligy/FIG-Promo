The short version

  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics script following you around.
  • Most visitors get no cookies at all.
  • The web app keeps your sign-in and settings in your browser, not in cookies.

What the website stores

  • Nothing, for most visitors. Browsing the site and joining the waitlist don't set cookies.
  • FIG team only: signing in to the admin page sets secure, HttpOnly cookies: one keeps you signed in for 8 hours, one remembers that browser for 90 days, one counts wrong tries, and short-lived ones (10 minutes at most) hold a sign-in or set-up step.

What the web app keeps

When you use FIG in your browser, it keeps your sign-in and your settings (like your language) in this browser's own storage, so you stay signed in and it remembers them. They're not cookies and they don't track you anywhere else. Signing out removes your sign-in; clearing this site's data in your browser removes the rest.

Things loaded from other services

The map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, the animation library from cdnjs and the map library from jsDelivr. They see a normal web request (like any website you visit) but we don't send them anything about you.
