Eating out with FIG

Claiming, codes, paying and takeout.

Is FIG free?

Yes, completely. Deals are free to claim and use.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page for as long as the deal runs. Show the QR code at the counter when you order, and staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. Your code changes every 5 minutes, so only the live code on your phone works.

What do I pay?

Just your bill, like normal, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app; that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners anything.

How do reviews work?

Only diners whose visit was scanned can review, so every review comes from a real visit. Rate a visit right after it, or later from Past visits on your Deals page.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Takeout orders go through the app and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, watch it get made, and pick it up. Some restaurants also take takeout at full price: tap Order takeout on their page. FIG doesn't deliver.

What if a restaurant won't honour a deal?

Report the visit in the app. A real person looks at every report and replies within 24 hours.

Running deals on FIG

Cost, scanning, deals and takeout.

What does FIG cost?

Nothing for dine-in. Joining, your page, deals and scans are free, with no commission on dine-in. On takeout orders diners pay for in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food (before tax), and Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan: open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming soon). Invite your staff in Settings: they scan with their own FIG sign-in.

What if a code won't scan?

Tap Enter code and type the 8 characters under the diner's QR. It runs the same check as a scan.

How do I post a deal?

In the app, open Deals and tap New. Pick the dishes (or the whole order, for dine-in), the offer, the days and hours, and check it the way diners will see it. You can pause it anytime, or post an ended deal again in one tap.

When does my page go live?

As soon as you finish setup: photos, address, hours and cuisine. Nothing to pay first.

Can diners order takeout?

Yes. Add your menu: first the sections, then the dishes in each. Turn on takeout and pick how many deals one order can use: 1, 2, 3, more or no limit, or 0 to take orders at full price. You scan their pickup code when they collect.

How do I stop?

There's nothing to cancel. Pause your deals, or close your account in Settings anytime.

Still stuck?

Message us and a real person replies within 24 hours. Or watch the quick tutorial videos for diners and restaurants.
