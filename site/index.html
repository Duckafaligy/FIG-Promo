Get your restaurant on FIG

Build your page, menu and first deal as drafts. It is free and takes a few minutes. When you are ready to publish, Stripe checks your ID and bank account (about 5 minutes) and FIG never sees your ID. Takeout also needs your HST number, which you can add later.

Questions owners ask.

Straight answers. More in the help center.

For restaurants

When can my restaurant join?

Today. Sign up on the web and build your page for free. Diners open on January 1, 2027, in Markham first, then Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills.

What does FIG cost restaurants?

Nothing to set up, publish, post deals or scan, for up to 2 locations. There is no commission on dine-in or on your bill. FIG earns only on takeout paid in the app: it keeps 5% of the food after any deal and before tax (plus 13% HST on that fee once FIG is registered for HST). Stripe's card fee, 2.9% + $0.30 on most Canadian cards, comes off the same payment, and the restaurant pays it. Publishing and takeout need Stripe's identity, business and bank checks. Takeout also needs your HST number and two-step sign-in. Stripe pays you out on its usual schedule, often within a few business days.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan. Open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming). Your staff sign in with their own FIG accounts.

When can I publish my page?

As soon as you finish Stripe's checks, which take about 5 minutes. Have your ID and a bank account ready. Until then, everything you build stays a draft that diners can't see. Add at least one photo to publish. Diners see published pages from January 1, 2027. An HST number is needed only for takeout.

For diners

When does FIG open for diners?

January 1, 2027, in Markham first, then Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills. People on the waitlist get in first, in the order they joined. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it opens.

Is FIG free?

Yes. Deals are free to claim and use, and you never pay FIG anything.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page while the deal runs. At the counter, show the QR code. Staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. A code works once. After staff scan it, it is used. If the deal can be used again, your phone shows a new code, so a screenshot just says "already used".

What do I pay?

Your bill, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app, and that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Restaurants see your first name and last initial, and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes, where a restaurant offers it. Pick your items and a pickup time up to 24 hours ahead. You can cancel until 20 minutes before pickup. FIG doesn't deliver.
