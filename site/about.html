Why FIG exists

“A full room at 7 pm, then a slow Tuesday afternoon.”

Markham is one of the best places in Canada to eat. Dim sum, ramen, Hakka, Hong Kong cafés, Korean fried chicken, Indian sweets, bubble tea on every plaza. Most of the places that make it great are small and independent.

Those owners all have the same problem: quiet hours. The usual answer is a delivery app, but delivery platforms take a percentage of every order, and a lot of owners decided long ago it doesn't add up.

FIG is built the other way around. The restaurant decides the deal, the days, the hours and how many a day. Diners walk in and pay the restaurant like normal. FIG takes no commission on deals or on the bill.

Restaurants use FIG for free. We make money only from 5% of the food on takeout orders diners pay for in the app.

For diners

  1. FindA live deal near you on the home feed or the map. Filter by craving, budget and diet (halal, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and more).
  2. ClaimIn one tap. It’s yours for as long as the deal runs.
  3. ShowYour code at the counter. It works once, so a screenshot can't be reused.
  4. RateThe visit afterwards, so reviews on FIG come from real visits.

Some deals also let you order takeout and pick up. You can send a restaurant's link to your group chat: it opens the place's page on FIG with its live deals, so everyone agrees on where to eat.

For restaurants

  1. Set upYour page: photos, address, hours, cuisine and dietary options.
  2. PublishAfter Stripe's quick identity and bank checks (about 5 minutes).
  3. PostA deal for the hours you want to fill, with an optional daily cap. Pause it anytime.
  4. ScanDiners at the counter with any phone or tablet. No fee per scan.

The dashboard shows scans, new diners and which deals work, so you can keep what fills seats and drop what doesn't.

What makes FIG different.

  • No commission on the bill.Diners pay you, at your counter. FIG is paid only from 5% of the food on takeout orders diners pay for in the app.
  • Honest deals.Every deal, timer and opening hour is set by the restaurant itself.
  • Codes that can't be copied.One-time QR codes stop screenshots and resold deals.
  • Private by default.Restaurants never see a diner's phone number, even for a takeout order.
  • Real reviews.Only diners whose visit was scanned can review, and paying FIG never changes or removes a review.
  • Local first.Unionville, Markham Village, Cornell, Milliken, Cathedraltown and the Highway 7 corridor first. Then expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills.

Questions people ask about FIG

Restaurant owners: see why FIG is free for restaurants. Everyone else: say hello, or read the blog.

What is FIG?

FIG is a restaurant deals app for Markham, Ontario. Restaurants post their own deals, diners find them on a map, claim one in a tap and show a code at the counter to save.

Is FIG free?

Yes for diners, always. Restaurants are free too, with no commission on the bill and no fee per diner. FIG earns only by keeping 5% of the food on takeout orders diners pay for in the app.

Where is FIG available?

FIG is launching in Markham, Ontario. Markham first, expanding to Richmond Hill and Vaughan Mills.

Who makes FIG?

A small team based in Markham, Ontario.

Is FIG a delivery app?

No. FIG brings diners into the restaurant. It’s a live-deals app: you eat in, or, when a deal allows it, takeout and pick up. You never pay FIG anything.
