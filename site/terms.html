1. Who we are

FIG is a local deals app based in Markham, Ontario, Canada, run by Brendan Lau ("FIG", "we", "us"). These terms cover the FIG app and website. By creating an account, you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy.

2. Your account

You can look around FIG without an account. To claim deals, order or post, you need one, and you must be 14 or older. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian that it's okay. Use your real name, an email (confirmed with a code) and, if you like, a mobile number (a contact number only: FIG never verifies it, texts it or uses it to sign you in). One account per person. Keep your password private. You can turn on two-step sign-in (an authenticator app or a code by email, with backup codes if you lose your phone). Restaurant owners and managers need it for money steps like payouts and refunds. Every account has a username. A staff account is an ordinary FIG account, made from an invite from a restaurant, and the person must be 18 or older. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

3. How deals work

Deals are offered by restaurants, not by FIG. Each deal shows its own rules (times, limits, dine-in only, and so on) before you claim it.

A claimed deal is yours until the restaurant ends the deal, or until a flash or dated deal is over.

At the restaurant, open your QR code. It doesn't change with time: a code works once, and if the deal can be used more than once, your screen shows a new code after each scan.

The deal is used when staff scan your code. That scan is a "redemption". A restaurant can let one claim be used more than once (up to 20 times) and can set a wait between uses; your claim shows how many uses are left and when the next one is ready.

Each deal can be claimed once per person per day, whether you claim it or order it as takeout. Scans of one claim on the same day count as one visit. If you pay for a takeout order after you've already used its deal that day, the order is declined and your card isn't charged.

Restaurants can change or end a deal at any time. A deal isn't guaranteed until staff scan it. Some deals come as takeout: a takeout order comes with a deal, which is applied to it, unless the restaurant sets 0 deals per order, then it is at full price; dine-in only deals can't be. You pay the restaurant in the app. If you don't collect an order within 2 hours of its pickup time, it shows as a missed pickup on your takeout record (12 months), which restaurants can see when you order. Nobody reports it and takeout is never switched off for it.

You choose when you collect a takeout order, from the earliest time the restaurant can make it up to 24 hours ahead, while the restaurant is open.

You can cancel a takeout order for a full refund at any time before the restaurant accepts it, and after that until 20 minutes before your pickup time. From 20 minutes before pickup the restaurant may already be making your food, so you can no longer cancel or be refunded through FIG. A cancelled order is refunded in full to the card you paid with; your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

4. Play fair

Restaurants honour every deal in good faith, so we protect them from abuse. Don't:

make more than one account

share, screenshot or sell QR codes

fake visits or redeem without buying

use bots, scripts or location spoofing

harass restaurant staff or other diners

If we see this, we may cancel claims or suspend your account.

5. Restaurants and your food

FIG doesn't run any restaurant. Food, prices, service, safety and allergens are the restaurant's responsibility. Dietary filters are a guide only. Always tell staff about allergies before you order.

6. What you send us

If you send us photos, reports or feedback, you still own them. You let us use them to run and improve FIG. Don't send anything that isn't yours or breaks the law. Reviews must be about your own scanned visit and honest. They're public under your first name and last initial. We can remove reviews that are abusive, fake or off-topic.

7. Leaving FIG

You can delete your account anytime in Profile → Account & password. We can suspend or close accounts that break these terms. If we do, we'll tell you why unless the law or safety stops us.

When you delete your account, restaurants keep their records of your orders and visits without your name. You can choose to keep a way to bring them back if you return within 12 months; see the Privacy policy.

8. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep FIG accurate and running, but it's provided "as is". As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses, and our total liability to you is limited to the greater of what you paid us in the last 12 months or $50 CAD. Nothing here removes your rights under Ontario consumer protection law.

9. Changes to these terms

If we make an important change, we'll tell you in the app at least 14 days before it starts. If you keep using FIG after that, the new terms apply.
