Free to join, $1.50 a scan: how FIG pricing works

FIG is free to join and to go live. Restaurants pay $1.50 only when a FIG diner's code is scanned, from a prepaid balance.

By the FIG team · Markham, Ontario

Key points

  • FIG is free to join and to go live. Every restaurant starts with 10 free scans.
  • After that, each verified scan of a FIG diner's code costs $1.50 CAD from a prepaid balance.
  • No commission on the bill, no monthly fee. Top up $20, $50, $100 or any amount, or turn on automatic top-up.
In this post

What you pay

Nothing to join, nothing to go live, and your first 10 scans are free. After that, you pay $1.50 CAD each time your staff scan a FIG diner's code at the counter: one customer FIG brought you, one charge. No commission on the bill, and no monthly fee.

A diner who uses your deal pays you, at your counter, the way they always do.

Your balance

Scans come out of a prepaid balance, shared by all your locations. Top up $20, $50, $100 or any amount from $10 to $500 by card through Stripe, or turn on automatic top-up (for example: when you drop under $5, add $20, up to a monthly limit you choose).

FIG lets you know when your balance drops under $10, under $5, and when it runs out. At $0 your page stays up, but new claims pause until you top up. Deals diners already claimed still work.

What's included

  • Unlimited deals, everyday and flash
  • Scanning on any phone or tablet, for every staff member
  • Takeout ordering, with diners paying you at pickup
  • Chats with diners (their phone number stays private)
  • A dashboard with scans, new diners and what's working

You control the cost

You only pay when a diner actually comes in. You choose the deal, the days and hours, and an optional daily cap, so a deal never brings more scans than you planned. Pause anytime. Read the full business terms.

Questions

How much does FIG cost a restaurant?

Nothing to join. 10 free scans, then $1.50 CAD per verified scan from a prepaid balance. No commission, no monthly fee.

Is there a contract?

No. There's no subscription to cancel. Top up when you want, and close your account anytime.
