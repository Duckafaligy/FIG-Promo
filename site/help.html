For diners

Is FIG free?

Yes. Deals are free to claim and use.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits on your Deals page while the deal runs. At the counter, show the QR code. Staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. A code works once. After staff scan it, it is used. If the deal can be used again, your phone shows a new code, so a screenshot just says "already used".

What do I pay?

Your bill, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app, and that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners.

How do reviews work?

Only diners whose visit was scanned can review, so every review comes from a real visit. Rate right after your visit, or later from Past visits on your Deals page.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Restaurants see your first name and last initial, and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes, where a restaurant offers it. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, choose a pickup time and pay in the app. FIG doesn't deliver.

When can I pick up my order?

You choose the pickup time, from the earliest time the restaurant can make it up to 24 hours ahead, while it is open.

Can I cancel a takeout order?

Yes. Cancel for a full refund any time before the restaurant accepts, and after that until 20 minutes before your pickup time. After that the kitchen may already be cooking, so you can't cancel through FIG. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, in full. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What if a restaurant won't honour a deal?

Report the visit in the app. A real person reads every report and replies within 24 hours.

Start here

Why do I see a quick check when I sign up?

It is a short check by hCaptcha that keeps bots out. It takes a few seconds and runs only when you sign up, sign in or reset a password. hCaptcha processes your internet address and browser signals only for that. Details are on the Privacy page.

Why do I connect Stripe?

Stripe is the payment company FIG uses. It checks who you are and pays your takeout money to your bank. Build your page, menu and deals first. You connect only to publish, or to turn on takeout. FIG never sees your ID or bank number. Stripe keeps them under its own privacy policy, and FIG keeps only whether and when Stripe verified your account.

Can I try FIG before connecting Stripe?

Yes. Build your page, menu and deals and invite your staff, all as drafts that diners can't see. Connect Stripe when you want to publish. It takes about 5 minutes, and you need your ID and a bank account.

When can I publish my page?

As soon as you finish Stripe's checks, which take about 5 minutes. Have your ID and a bank account ready. Until then, everything you build stays a draft that diners can't see. Add at least one photo to publish. Diners see published pages from January 1, 2027. An HST number is needed only for takeout.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan. Open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming). Your staff sign in with their own FIG accounts.

Deals and scanning

How do I post a deal?

Open Deals and tap New deal. Pick the dishes (or the whole order, for dine-in), the offer, and the days and hours. Check it the way diners will see it. You can pause a deal any time, or post an ended one again in one tap.

What if a code won't scan?

Tap Enter code and type the 8 characters under the diner's QR. It runs the same check as a scan.

What if a scan looks wrong?

Message us from Help within 14 days. We review it, and if it was a mistake we remove it.

Takeout

Can diners order takeout?

Yes, once takeout is on. Add your menu (sections first, then dishes), then choose how many deals one order can use: 1, 2, 3, more, no limit, or 0 for full-price orders. Diners pay in the app and choose a pickup time. The card is charged when you accept, and nothing is charged if you decline. Scan their pickup code when they collect.

Can a diner cancel a takeout order?

Yes. A diner can cancel an accepted order for a full refund until 20 minutes before pickup, and you are told right away. The refund includes FIG's fee on that order. Stripe's own fee is not returned. After that the order is locked in and only you can refund it. Accepting an order keeps the pickup time the diner chose.

Can I pause takeout in a rush?

Yes. Tap Takeout: On on your dashboard or Orders to pause new orders for 20 or 40 minutes, or until tomorrow's opening, or to add prep time. Orders already in aren't touched, and takeout comes back on by itself.

Can I refund an order?

Yes, owners and managers can: open the order and tap Refund, for all of it or part. FIG gives back its fee in proportion; Stripe keeps its card fee.

Money

What does FIG cost?

Nothing to start. FIG is free for restaurants, and earns only 5% of the food on takeout paid in the app.

FreeSigning up, drafts, publishing (after Stripe's checks), scanning, deals, your dashboard and insights, for up to 2 locations for now. No commission on dine-in or on your bill.
FIG keeps5% of the food after any deal and before tax, on takeout paid in the app only. Plus 13% HST on that fee once FIG is registered for HST.
Stripe keepsIts card fee, 2.9% + $0.30 for most Canadian cards, from the same payment. The restaurant pays it.
  • Publishing and takeout both need Stripe's identity, business and bank checks. Takeout also needs your HST number, two-step sign-in and the takeout switch.
  • Stripe pays the rest out to your bank on its usual schedule, often within a few business days.
  • The HST on a takeout order is yours to remit.
How do I stop?

There's nothing to cancel, because FIG is free for restaurants. Pause your deals, turn takeout off or close your account in Settings any time.

Staff and security

How do I add my staff?

In Settings, invite a person by email or with a link that expires. They open it, see which restaurant and role it is for, and tap Accept or Decline. Nobody joins your team without accepting. Roles are Owner, Manager and Staff, and you can remove people or cancel an invite anytime.

What is two-step sign-in, and who needs it?

It is a second code when you sign in, from an authenticator app or sent by email. Owners and managers need it, with a recent code, for money steps: payouts, turning takeout on, refunds, handing a restaurant over and closing it. Backup codes help if you lose your phone.
