Takeout with a deal, pick up, skip the delivery fees

Some FIG deals let you order takeout from a Markham restaurant's menu, watch it get made and pick it up, with no delivery or service fees.

By the FIG team · Markham, Ontario · 2 min read

Key points

  • Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, and pick it up.
  • No delivery fee and no service fee from FIG.
  • Some restaurants also take takeout at full price, with no deal needed. FIG doesn’t deliver.

How it works

Find a deal marked Dine in or takeout and tap Takeout. The menu opens with the deal applied; add what you want. The restaurant accepts it and you can watch its status: new, cooking, ready. Head over, show your pickup code and go.

Why pickup is cheaper

There's no driver, so there's no delivery fee, and FIG doesn't add a service fee. You pay the menu price, minus the deal.

It starts with a deal

Only deals the restaurant allows to be ordered takeout have the button. Dine-in only deals are for eating in.

Private by default

The restaurant never sees your phone number. If you need to change something, message them from the order in the app.

Questions

Does FIG deliver?

No. You takeout with a deal and pick the food up yourself.

Is there a fee for takeout on FIG?

No. FIG doesn't charge diners anything: no delivery or service fees.

Find a deal near you.

FIG is free for diners. Opens in Markham on January 1, 2027.
