Privacy policy

The short version

  • We collect what we need to show you deals nearby and check that redemptions are real.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Restaurants only see what they need to verify your visit.
  • Location is used while you use FIG, and for alerts only if you turn them on.
  • You can download or delete your data anytime.

1. Who's responsible

FIG Technologies Inc. in Ontario, Canada is responsible for your personal information. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA). Our Privacy Officer answers questions at privacy@fig.app.

2. What we collect

• Account: name, username, email and mobile number (each confirmed with a code), photo, and your password (stored scrambled, never readable).

• Taste: cravings, budget, dietary needs and home area from Profile setup.

• Location: your precise location while you use FIG, to show nearby deals and confirm you're at the restaurant when you activate a deal or pick up an order. We keep the result of that check with the visit record. If you turn on arrival reminders, also when you arrive at a restaurant where you have a claimed deal.

• Activity: claims, redemptions, stamps, searches, takeout orders, chat messages, and the reviews and photos you post.

• Device: phone model, app version, notification token and crash reports.

• Purchases: Apple or Google tells us if Premium is active. We never see your card.

• Support: messages and reports you send us.

3. How we use it

• Show deals and places near you

• Verify redemptions and stop fraud

• Count stamps and the free Premium you spend them on

• Personalise Home and AI picks (you can turn this off)

• Send the alerts and emails you chose

• Deliver chat messages between you and a restaurant. Phone numbers typed into a chat are hidden. The FIG team reads a chat only when someone reports it

• Answer support requests and improve FIG

We don't sell your information or show you third-party ads based on it.

4. Dietary needs

Dietary needs can say something about your health or beliefs, so we treat them as sensitive. They're only used to filter deals for you. They're never shared with restaurants.

5. What restaurants see

When staff scan your QR, the restaurant sees your first name and last initial, the deal, and the time. Their dashboard shows totals, like "42 redemptions this week", not who you are. Restaurants never see your email, location or history. They never see your mobile number, not even for a takeout order: they reach you by chat or a notification in FIG. Reviews you post are public and show your first name and last initial.

6. Companies that help us

These companies process data for us, only to run FIG, under contracts that protect it:

• Supabase: database and sign-in

• Vercel: hosting

• Stripe: restaurant payments

• Apple and Google: sign-in, maps, notifications, Premium billing

• Anthropic: AI picks

7. AI picks and your data

For AI picks, we send Anthropic's Claude your taste settings, what you type, and the deals near you. We don't send your name or email, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models.

8. Where your data lives

Some of these companies store data outside Canada, mostly in the United States. There, it's protected by our contracts but also covered by local law.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we erase your personal information within 30 days. Redemption records are kept without your name for up to 7 years, because restaurants are billed for them and tax law requires it.

10. Your choices

• See or fix your info in Profile

• Download it in Privacy & terms

• Turn off location, alerts or personalised picks

• Delete your account at any time

Not happy with our answer? You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Keeping it safe

We encrypt data in transit, scramble passwords and limit who on our team can access data. No system is perfect. If a breach could put you at risk, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.

12. Age

FIG is for people 14 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 14 has an account, we delete it.

13. Changes

If we change how we use your information in an important way, we'll tell you in the app first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

The website waitlist

When you join the waitlist on this website we keep your email or mobile number, whether you're a diner or a restaurant, the area you picked, when you joined and which link brought you here. We use it only to tell you when FIG opens near you and to send FIG news, because you ticked the consent box. Every email has an unsubscribe link and you can reply STOP to any text. We never sell it or share it with restaurants or advertisers.
