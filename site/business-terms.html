Business terms

The short version

  • One plan: $50 CAD a year for all your locations. No fee per scan, no other fees.
  • Your page shows to diners while your plan is on. Cancel anytime: it runs to the end of the year you paid for.
  • Honour your live deals exactly as written.
  • Only your enrolled staff devices can verify scans.

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to restaurants in Canada that use FIG Business. They sit alongside our Terms of service and Privacy policy. By signing up, you confirm you're 18 or older and can accept them for your business.

2. Your listing

You must run a real, licensed food business at the address you give us, and keep your details accurate. Your page goes live as soon as you finish setting it up. We can pause or remove a listing that breaks these terms.

3. Your deals

You choose your deals, times and daily limits. While a deal is live, you must honour it exactly as listed. Don't raise prices to cancel out a discount. If you pause or end a deal, honour claims made before that until they expire (up to 24 hours).

4. Your plan

• FIG for restaurants costs $50 CAD a year, plus applicable tax. One plan covers every location on your account.

• It includes unlimited deals and unlimited verified scans (valid QR scans by your enrolled devices), takeout orders, reviews and your numbers.

• There are no fees per scan, and no sign-up, monthly or listing fees.

5. Paying, renewing and cancelling

You pay by card through Stripe, which keeps your card details; FIG never sees them. Your plan renews each year on the date you started it, at the price then in effect, until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Plan in FIG Business: your plan runs to the end of the year you've paid for, then your page and deals stop showing to diners. If a payment fails, Stripe tries again for a short while and your page stays live; if it still fails, your plan ends. Receipts are in Plan (Manage billing). Takeout orders don't cost extra: you collect the food payment yourself, and you're responsible for your menu, prices and tax.

6. Wrong scans and disputes

You can report a scan you think is wrong within 14 days. We review it and remove it if it was a mistake. Duplicate scans are removed automatically.

7. Reviews

Paying for FIG never changes, hides or removes reviews. You can reply to reviews in public. Don't offer anything in exchange for a review.

8. Staff and devices

You're responsible for scans made by your staff and devices. Remove access when someone leaves.

9. Diner information

You'll see a diner's first name, last initial and the deal they used, never their mobile number or email. Reach a diner only through FIG (chat, or "We're waiting" on a takeout order), and only to serve that deal or order. Don't contact diners outside FIG, and handle their information as Canadian privacy law requires.

10. Ending the agreement

You can close your business account anytime. Your plan ends at once and the rest of the year isn't refunded. We can suspend or close your account if you break these terms, for example by not honouring deals or faking scans. If we close it for any other reason, we refund the unused months.

11. Responsibility

You're responsible for your food, prices, service and safety, and for any claims about them. FIG is provided "as is". As far as the law allows, our total liability is limited to the greater of the fees you paid us in the last 12 months or $100 CAD.

12. Changes and law

We'll give you at least 30 days' notice before changing the price. A new price applies from your next renewal. These terms follow Ontario law, and disputes go to the courts of Ontario.
