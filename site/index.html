Good questions.

Straight answers. More in the help center.

For diners

When does FIG open?

January 1, 2027, in Markham first, with Richmond Hill next. You can sign up and look around today, on the web at fig-promo.vercel.app/app.

Is FIG free?

Yes, completely. Deals are free to claim and use, and you never pay FIG anything.

How do I use a deal?

Claim it in the app. It waits in your Voucher tab for as long as the deal runs. Show the QR code at the counter when you order, and staff scan it or type the 8 characters under it.

Can someone use my screenshot?

No. Your code changes every 5 minutes, so only the live code on your phone works.

What do I pay?

Just your bill, like normal, with the deal taken off. Some restaurants let you pay for takeout in the app; that money goes to the restaurant. FIG never charges diners anything.

Do restaurants see my phone number?

Never. Chats and takeout orders go through the app and your number stays private.

Can I order takeout?

Yes. Tap Takeout on a deal that allows it, add what you want, watch it get made, and pick it up. Some restaurants also take takeout at full price: tap Order takeout on their page. FIG doesn't deliver.

For restaurants

What does FIG cost restaurants?

Nothing for dine-in. Joining, your page, deals, scans and chats are free, with no commission on dine-in. On takeout orders diners pay for in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food (before tax), and Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment. FIG Premium is optional, $50 a year.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Any phone, tablet or computer can scan: open FIG on the web (the iPhone and Android apps are coming soon). Add staff and their devices in Settings.

When does my page go live?

As soon as you finish setup: photos, address, hours and cuisine. Nothing to pay first.
