How FIG makes money: 5% of in-app takeout

FIG is free for restaurants, with no commission on dine-in and no fee per diner. FIG earns only on takeout orders diners pay for in the app: it keeps 5% of the food.

Key takeaways

  • Free for restaurants: your page, unlimited deals, every scan and insights cost nothing, for up to 2 locations for now.
  • There's no upfront payment, no monthly or yearly fee and nothing to choose or cancel.
  • No commission and no fee per diner on dine-in. On takeout paid in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food, and Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment.
  • On a $30 takeout order, FIG's 5% is $1.50 and Stripe's card fee is about $1.28. You keep about $27.22 of the $30, plus the HST you remit.
  • Paying FIG never changes your search position, your reviews or your deal.

The short answer

FIG is free for restaurants. Everything a restaurant needs to bring diners in costs nothing: your page, your menu, unlimited everyday and flash deals, scanning diners at the counter and the insights that show what's working, for up to 2 locations for now. A diner who walks in with your deal pays you directly, and FIG takes nothing from that bill.

FIG earns in one way: it keeps 5% of the food on takeout orders that diners pay for in the app. This post goes through it, with the arithmetic.

No contract, nothing to cancel

There is no contract. You can pause your deals or close your account whenever you like, and takeout orders already paid are paid out as usual. The full rules are in the business terms.

Takeout paid in the app: 5% of the food

When a diner orders takeout and pays in the app, the payment goes through Stripe. FIG keeps 5% of the food before tax, Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment, and the rest, including the HST, is paid out to you. The HST is yours to remit, as with any sale. FIG isn't registered for HST yet; once it is, it will add 13% HST to its 5% fee and tell restaurants at least 30 days before that starts.

Stripe's rate is its published price for Canadian domestic cards. Its fee is charged on the full payment, tax included.

Takeout on FIG is paid in the app, so it needs two things in Settings → Get paid: your HST number (it goes on every takeout receipt, with your restaurant as the seller) and Stripe payouts. Dine-in deals need neither. Each month's takeout statement shows sales, HST, fees and what you kept.

Why not charge per diner, like other apps?

Because the restaurant already gave something up: the deal itself. A 2-for-1 or 20% off is the price of the visit. Charging again for the same diner would push owners to make deals smaller and rarer, which is worse for everyone, diners included. FIG wins when deals are good enough to post, so it doesn't tax them: there is no fee per diner, however many come.

What paying FIG never does

  • Change your deal or its price.
  • Move you up in search or on the map.
  • Hide, remove or reorder reviews. Only diners whose visit was scanned can review, and you can reply in public.
  • Share a diner's phone number or email with you. Orders and reviews show their first name and last initial.

The full rules are in the business terms.

How this compares

If you're weighing FIG against a delivery app, compare the job, not just the rate. Delivery apps bring the food to the door and charge for it: Uber Eats and DoorDash publish Canadian delivery plans between 20% and 30% per order. FIG brings the diner to your door for free, with nothing per diner. For pickup, the big apps charge 8% to 10% of an order (Uber Eats charges 15% if your in-store prices aren't validated), which is close to what FIG's 5% plus the card fee comes to. Getting started

Sign up as a business, add your menu and draft your first deal. Setting up is free, and everything stays a draft until you finish Stripe's identity, business and bank checks to publish. When you're ready for takeout paid in the app, add your HST number and connect Stripe in Settings → Get paid. Questions? Restaurant help or message us.

Questions

How much does FIG cost a restaurant?

Nothing. Signing up, building your page, menu and deals, publishing them, scanning and your dashboard are free, for up to 2 locations for now. On takeout orders diners pay for in the app, FIG keeps 5% of the food before tax, and Stripe's card fee comes off the same payment.

Is there a commission on dine-in?

No. A diner who uses your deal pays you at the counter, and FIG takes nothing from that bill. There's no fee per diner.

Who pays Stripe's fee on takeout?

It comes off the same in-app payment, before the payout: 2.9% + $0.30 on Canadian cards, Stripe's published rate. On a $33.90 payment that's about $1.28.

Does FIG collect HST for me?

The diner pays the HST with the order and it's paid out to you with the rest. It's yours to remit. Your HST number goes on every takeout receipt, with your restaurant as the seller.

Is there a contract?

No. There's no contract and nothing to cancel. You can pause your deals or close your account whenever you like, and takeout orders already paid are paid out as usual.

Can I pay to rank higher?

No. Nothing you pay FIG changes where you appear in search or on the map, or what your reviews say.
