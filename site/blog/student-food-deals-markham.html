Student food deals in Markham: eating out on a budget

How high school and university students in Markham can eat out for less: after-school deals, group plans and budget filters.

By the FIG team · For diners · October 4, 2026 · 2 min read
In this post

Key takeaways

  • After-school hours are exactly when many restaurants are quiet, so that's when deals show up.
  • Set a budget filter in FIG once and only see deals you can afford.
  • Share a deal to your group chat so everyone can agree on where to go.

After school is deal time

Between 3 and 6 pm, a lot of Markham restaurants and bubble tea shops are quiet. That's when owners post deals, and when you're most likely to be hungry.

Set your budget once

When you sign up, tell FIG your budget and what you crave. The home feed and map then show deals that fit, nearest first, so you're not scrolling past $40 dinners.

Decide with your friends

Can't agree where to eat? Share a deal from FIG straight to your group chat. Everyone sees the same place, the same deal and how far it is.

Free is better

FIG is free for diners. Nothing to collect, nothing to upgrade: just deals.

Ask before you go

Not sure if there's space for six, or if you can order the deal ahead? Message the restaurant from the deal. Your phone number stays private.

Questions

Is FIG free for students?

Yes. FIG is free for every diner, with nothing to upgrade.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You claim deals in the app and pay the restaurant directly at the counter.
