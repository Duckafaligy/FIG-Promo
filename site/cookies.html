The short version

  • No advertising cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies on this website only if you accept them; if you say no thanks, it sets none.
  • The website keeps a few small notes in your browser's storage, listed below. They are not cookies.
  • The web app keeps your sign-in and settings in your browser, not in cookies.

What the website stores

  • Google Analytics cookies, only if you accept. The first time you visit, a notice asks whether FIG may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages help. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga, and _ga_ followed by an ID) that count visits and pages viewed, kept up to 2 years; Google gets your approximate location and device type, not your name. If you say no thanks, or don't answer, nothing is loaded from Google and no cookie is set. Change your mind anytime with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page: choosing no thanks there deletes the cookies. You can also block them in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
  • No advertising cookies. FIG doesn't set any.
  • Vercel Web Analytics. It also counts visits, and sets no cookies.

Small notes in your browser

These are kept in your browser's own storage, not in cookies, and stay on your device:

  • fig.consent is your answer about Google Analytics cookies. It stays until you change it or clear this site's data.
  • fig.src remembers which link brought you here, so a creator gets credit if you join the waitlist. It's gone when you close the tab.
  • fig.paused remembers that you paused the animations on the home page. It's gone when you close the tab.
  • fig.dinersOpen remembers whether FIG is open to diners, so the web app's loading screen is right. It stays until you clear this site's data.
  • fig.reloadedForDeploy stops the web app from reloading over and over after an update. It's gone when you close the tab.

What the web app keeps

When you use FIG in your browser, it keeps your sign-in and your settings (like your language) in this browser's own storage, so you stay signed in and it remembers them. They're not cookies and they don't track you anywhere else. It also keeps an offline copy of the pages and pictures you've opened, so repeat visits open faster. Signing out removes your sign-in; clearing this site's data in your browser removes the rest.

Things loaded from other services

The map tiles come from OpenFreeMap and the map library from jsDelivr; the animation library is served from FIG's own site. They see a normal web request (like any website you visit) but we don't send them anything about you. Google's script loads only if you accept Google Analytics, as described above.
